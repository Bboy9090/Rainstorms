# Rainstorms Examples

This folder contains sample outputs generated by Rainstorms to showcase the app's capabilities.

## 📚 Sample Book Exports

These are complete storybook PDFs generated through the full Rainstorms pipeline (Idea Lab → Blueprint → Characters → Pages → Export):

- **`robot-emotions.pdf`** — *"A brave little robot discovers emotions"*
  A 12-page picture book about BeeBot, a utility robot who learns to feel through friendship with Luna, a young inventor.

- **`captain-blanket.pdf`** — *"Captain Blanket and the Midnight Brother"*
  The demo story included with Rainstorms. A 10-page adventure about Oliver who transforms his blanket into a superhero cape to help his baby brother Max sleep through the night.

> **Note:** These PDF files will be added once generated. The story content and structure are complete — placeholder files indicate where the final exports will be placed.

## How These Were Created

All sample books were generated using:

1. **Idea Lab** — Story concept, tone, age range, page count
2. **Story Blueprint** — AI-generated title, hook, theme, outline
3. **Character Forge** — Named characters with personalities and visual traits
4. **Page Builder** — Page-by-page text and illustration prompts
5. **Publishing Center** — PDF export with metadata

No manual writing or illustration was done — these are 100% AI-assisted outputs from the Rainstorms platform, demonstrating real production-quality results.
