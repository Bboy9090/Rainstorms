## Summary

Oliver discovers his old baby blanket transforms into a magical cape when the clock strikes bedtime. When shadow monsters threaten his baby brother Max's peaceful sleep, Captain Blanket springs into action! Through imagination, courage, and brotherly love, Oliver learns that being a hero isn't about being fearless — it's about protecting the ones you love.

## Characters

### Oliver (Captain Blanket) — Main
- **Personality:** Brave, imaginative, loving, protective; sometimes scared but always pushes through
- **Appearance:** 5-year-old boy, messy brown hair, bright eyes, rosy cheeks, blue star pajamas + shimmering silver-blue cape
- **Special Trait:** His love for Max makes the cape glow with golden light

### Baby Max — Supporting
- **Personality:** Sweet, innocent, peaceful; giggles easily, reaches for Oliver
- **Appearance:** Chubby 10-month-old, wispy blonde hair, big blue eyes, yellow duck onesie
- **Special Trait:** His innocent smile can light up any dark room

### Shadow Monsters — Minor
- **Personality:** Mischievous but not evil — scatter when confronted with love
- **Appearance:** Wispy purple-gray shapes, big cartoonish yellow eyes, constantly shifting
- **Special Trait:** Shrink when exposed to light or love

### The Nightmare King — Minor
- **Personality:** Dramatic, pompous, powerless against love
- **Appearance:** Large shadow, crooked crown of darkness, exaggerated frown
- **Special Trait:** Melts into harmless starlight when defeated
