# Demo Project: Captain Blanket and the Midnight Brother

## Overview

This demo showcases what a complete Rainstorms project looks like. Users can explore it by clicking "Try Demo Project" on the home screen.

## Story Details

**Title:** Captain Blanket and the Midnight Brother

**Hook:** When darkness falls, one brave big brother discovers that the greatest superpower is love.

**Summary:** Oliver discovers his old baby blanket transforms into a magical cape when the clock strikes bedtime. When shadow monsters threaten his baby brother Max's peaceful sleep, Captain Blanket springs into action! Through imagination, courage, and brotherly love, Oliver learns that being a hero isn't about being fearless—it's about protecting the ones you love.

**Theme:** The power of love and protection between siblings

**Tone:** Cozy, adventurous, bedtime calm

**Age Range:** 3-8 years

**Page Count:** 10

## Characters

### Oliver (Captain Blanket) — Main
- **Personality:** Brave, imaginative, loving, protective
- **Appearance:** 5-year-old boy, messy brown hair, bright curious eyes, blue star pajamas, shimmering silver-blue cape
- **Special Trait:** His love makes the cape glow with golden light

### Baby Max — Supporting
- **Personality:** Sweet, innocent, peaceful
- **Appearance:** Chubby 10-month-old, wispy blonde hair, big blue eyes, yellow duck onesie
- **Special Trait:** Innocent smile lights up dark rooms

### Shadow Monsters — Minor
- **Personality:** Mischievous but not evil
- **Appearance:** Wispy purple-gray shapes, cartoonish yellow eyes, constantly shifting
- **Special Trait:** Shrink when exposed to light or love

### The Nightmare King — Minor
- **Personality:** Dramatic, pompous, powerless against love
- **Appearance:** Larger shadow with crooked crown, exaggerated frown
- **Special Trait:** Melts into harmless starlight when defeated

## Page Outline

1. Oliver in bed, hears Max fussing, reaches for blanket
2. Blanket transforms into magical cape — Captain Blanket!
3. Captain Blanket sees shadow monsters around Max's crib
4. Shadows try to steal dreams, Captain Blanket stands firm
5. Cape creates shield of golden light, shadows retreat
6. Nightmare King appears
7. Oliver remembers loving moments with Max
8. Love defeats Nightmare King (melts to starlight)
9. Max sleeps peacefully, Captain Blanket pats him
10. Oliver hangs up cape, snuggles in bed, being a big brother is the best superpower

## Why This Story Works

- **Relatable protagonist:** Many children have baby siblings
- **Safe scares:** Monsters are silly, not terrifying
- **Clear arc:** Fear → Courage → Victory → Peace
- **Theme resonates:** Sibling love, protection, growing up
- **Perfect for bedtime:** Ends with cozy comfort
